This is a new squad for Into the Breach. Just extract the zip and put it right in the game's mods folder.

Feedback is preferred on the relevant channels of the official discord (or the specialized thread if that's still available): https://discord.gg/xRxPAb87JW

Check the Issues tab for known bugs that are being worked on.

Looking for another of my squads? You can find it here: https://github.com/Hederarch/ItB-Shell-Corps

**IMPORTANT**: This squad **WILL NOT WORK** without RedactedRice's Memhack extension! Get it here: https://github.com/Redacted-Rice/ITB-RedactedRiceExts/releases/tag/v2.0.0

*(also these do require the Into the Breach modloader. That shouldn't be a problem for most people but download it if you don't have it.)*

## What's Included
This mod adds a new squad which play dirty by risking grid and failing missions to gain extra resources. Save the corporate islands, or destroy and pillage them for your mysterious master.

<img width="342" height="317" alt="dreadwatch_menu" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c27e9b35-5ecd-440c-8eb6-79f1745ef3da" />

The squad can get additional objectives from their Handler through an unauthorized connection to the islands' Grid:

<img width="397" height="410" alt="wiretaps_demo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7beb3f59-c408-4f9c-a5d9-c14cbaa75185" />

If you risk *all* your grid, you can get an extra power core each island!

The **Baron Mech** can fly and punch over long distances, playing more like a backline artillery than a melee Prime mech.

<img width="359" height="312" alt="dislocator_demo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7f1d6b37-4d1c-46f5-8400-22a6ac8c56c8" />

- Phasing (1 core): Can project punches past multiple obstacles.
- +1 Damage (2 cores): Increases Damage by 1.

The **Sledge Mech** is a tough slab of a tank which can bounce its shots. Straight or single target pushes often require self-damage.

<img width="397" height="374" alt="crasher_demo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/43988a7f-bfe4-4469-bd80-10dd1dffb338" />

- Flak Catcher (2 cores): When shooting yourself, shoot again.
- Ally Immune (2 cores): The first hit can pass through allies and buildings. The next hit still damages everyone.

And the Tripod Mech pulls in targets with high-damage needle missiles. It's the squad's best option for direct kills.

<img width="397" height="340" alt="clawrockets_demo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9f7a800e-6421-4829-9d19-b76ebb12cb86" />

- +1 Damage (2 cores): Increases Damage by 1.
- +1 Damage (2 cores): Increases Damage by 1.
